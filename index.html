01 / OVERVIEW

A legacy built for the future.

Basaveshwar Engineering College (BEC) is a premier government-aided autonomous institution under the prestigious B.V.V. Sangha.

We provide a transformative education that combines rigorous academics with practical research. Our campus is a hub for innovation, recognized by AICTE and permanently affiliated with VTU, Belagavi.
