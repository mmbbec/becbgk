01 / ABOUT

A legacy built for the future.

Basaveshwar Engineering College, Bagalkote, is a premier technical institution established in 1963 and built around education, research, innovation and social impact.

From a modest beginning in 1963, BEC has developed into a multidisciplinary autonomous engineering institution serving generations of students from Karnataka and beyond.

The institution is government-aided, recognized by AICTE, permanently affiliated to Visvesvaraya Technological University, Belagavi, and accredited with an NAAC 'A' Grade.
